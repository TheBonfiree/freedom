Selected work

Features (4)   Commercials (3)   Broadcast (2)

The Salt House · feature · 1:12 · 14 sets · 2024

A tidal village built on rollers so the whole street could be flooded to the doorstep and drained overnight. Fourteen sets, nine of them rebuilt at least once during the shoot.

Nettlefield · feature · 1:12 · 22 puppets · 2024

Twenty-two puppets with replacement-face libraries of 340 pieces, printed in-house and hand-finished. Two spare bodies per principal, which is what got the shoot to picture lock.

Ferrous · commercial · 1:6 · 3 weeks · 2023

A foundry interior at 1:6 with practical fire, shot in-camera because the client wanted the sparks to sit behind the smoke rather than in front of it.

Harrow & Vine · commercial · 1:12 · 2 sets · 2023

A greengrocer's shop with 1,400 individually dressed items of stock, because the board called for a slow push-in and there was nowhere to hide.

Low Tide · broadcast · 1:12 · 6 sets · 2022

Six harbour sets on a rolling water table, with a rain bar rigged above the camera so the drops read at scale rather than as falling marbles.

The Orrery · feature · 1:8 · 1 hero build · 2022

A working brass orrery, geared so it could be driven a frame at a time and land on the same position after a week of shooting.

Pilgrim Street · broadcast · 1:12 · 11 sets · 2021

A terrace built to be shot from both sides, with removable ceilings and a lighting grid the gaffer could reach without a ladder.

Cinderheart · feature · 1:12 · 18 puppets · 2020

Eighteen puppets in foam latex over ball-and-socket armatures, with silicone hands cast thin enough to hold a match.

Marlowe's Ark · commercial · 1:24 · 1 set · 2019

A ship's hull at 1:24, forced-perspective to read at three times its length, on a gimbal driven by hand between frames.
