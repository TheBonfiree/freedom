Selected work

Drama (4)   Documentary (2)   Archive (2)
[1]Drama · 2024

The Quiet Border

Six-part serial shot across two winters on live roads. Dialogue was recovered rather than replaced — ADR only where a performance demanded it. Final mix 5.1.

[2]Drama · 2023

Meridian House

Period interior piece. Room tone was built from a single house recorded over a weekend, then treated per decade so the same walls aged with the story.

[3]Documentary · 2023

Cold Store

Feature documentary inside a working fish plant. The machinery was the score; the edit was cut to let it breathe rather than ducking it under narration.

[4]Archive · 2022

Tape 114 — ERR Restoration

Forty hours of quarter-inch broadcast material transferred and conformed. Restoration held deliberately light: hiss reduced, nothing else touched.

[5]Drama · 2022

Nightschool

Half-hour comedy drama with a laughter-free mix. The whole show sits on one ambience bed per location, which kept the cut points invisible.

[6]Documentary · 2021

The Long Ferry

Shot entirely on a Baltic crossing. Engine noise was recorded at four points in the hull so the mix could move the audience through the ship, not around it.

[7]Drama · 2020

Saaremaa Lights

Island-set thriller delivered in Atmos and 5.1 from a single premix. Foley was directed on location floors rather than a stage, and it shows.

[8]Archive · 2019

Children's Hour, 1974–79

A children's strand rebuilt from incomplete transmission tapes. Missing links were reconstructed from continuity scripts and the original studio ambience.
